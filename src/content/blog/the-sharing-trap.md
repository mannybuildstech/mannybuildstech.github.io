> AI-free post. 🚫🤖

Many times I have fallen into a dysfunctional cycle: 

Pick up a new interest --> "ohh I can be an influencer in this area" --> get lost in media production / marketing / planning --> lose the joy of it --> turns out I don't want to do it anymore.

You can be doing literally anything like plucking weeds in your yard, and a miniature **GaryVee** pops into you mind: "Hey buddy start documenting and sharing, you never know what could happen!"

At first glance it seems to make sense, you're putting in the time to learn something new and create - might as well document it and share it, and now you have the potential for revenue.

You spent a few hours learning harmonica and it brought you joy, and now you jumped the gun, created 5 social media accounts called **HarmoniBro** before you can play mary had a little lamb.

Something distasteful and toxic starts to creep in: you start to care more about the media / marketing department of your barely even started hobby THAN the actual meat and bones.
